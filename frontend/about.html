❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this platform free to use? ▾
Yes, all public features — including AI detection, fraud database search, and scam reporting — are completely free for everyone.
How is the fraud database maintained? ▾
Entries come from community reports, on-chain analysis, and partnerships with security researchers. All reports are manually reviewed by our moderation team before being published.
How accurate is the AI detection? ▾
Our AI uses rule-based pattern matching and heuristics tuned specifically for Web3/crypto scams. It detects the most common patterns. Always combine AI results with your own research.
Can I use the API for my project? ▾
Yes! We offer a public REST API. See the /api endpoints documentation for available routes. Rate limits apply to prevent abuse.
How do I deploy this platform myself? ▾
The platform supports one-click Docker deployment. Copy the .env.example to .env, configure your secrets, then run docker compose up -d.
