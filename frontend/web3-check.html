🛡 Web3 Safety Tips

  • Never share your seed phrase or private key with anyone
  • Always verify contract addresses on official project websites
  • Check token approval limits — revoke unnecessary permissions
  • Use hardware wallets for large holdings
  • Be wary of tokens that promise guaranteed returns
  • Check if you can sell a token before buying (honeypot test)
  • Verify NFT contracts before minting or buying
  • Use multiple wallets — separate trading from storage

⚠️ Red Flags

Anonymous team with no public profiles
Locked liquidity for very short periods only
Contract functions allowing owners to blacklist holders
Extremely high transaction taxes (>10%)
